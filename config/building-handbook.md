# South Heights Apartments – Resident Handbook

South Heights Apartments is a made-up building. Everything in this handbook is invented.

## Bins

- General waste: put bins out on Tuesday night.
- Recycling: put bins out every second Thursday night.

## Parking

- Each apartment has one parking bay.
- Visitor bays can be used for up to 4 hours, between 8 am and 8 pm.

## Laundry room

- Level 1.
- Open 7 am to 9 pm.

## Gym

- Level 2.
- Open 5 am to 10 pm.

## Pool

- Level 2.
- Open 6 am to 9 pm.

## Moving in and out, and booking the lift

- Book the lift at least 48 hours ahead with the building manager.
- Moves can happen Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 4 pm.

## Noise

- Quiet hours are 10 pm to 7 am.

## Pets

- Pets are allowed with a written request to the building manager.
- Pets must be kept on a lead in shared areas.

## Reporting a repair

- Report repairs to the building manager by email or phone.
- Report urgent repairs by phone.
